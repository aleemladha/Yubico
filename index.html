Know what
protects you.

A security key is one part of the system. These are the boundaries that matter.

Where does the private key live?

The generation commands ask the YubiKey to create the private key on-device. Only the public key and certificate are exported. Windows keeps a provider association and delegates private-key operations to the card. Non-exportable does not mean invulnerable to every hardware or firmware attack. Slot status alone cannot prove that a pre-existing key was never imported.

Touch is consent, not a fingerprint

Anyone can touch a standard YubiKey contact. Touch-only prevents silent use while the key waits for contact, but someone holding it can approve an operation. PIN plus touch adds a knowledge check. PIN verification can be cached with the “once” policy. These controls do not protect files from malware while the volume is unlocked.

Factory defaults are not your final policy

Standard slots 9a and 9d default to requiring a PIN; slot 9e normally does not. Touch is normally not required. The factory PIV PIN is publicly known. Configure your PIN and management-key protection deliberately, inspect the actual policies, and never guess unknown credentials. Do not reset a working PIV application.

Your recovery password is another key

The BitLocker recovery password unlocks without the YubiKey, PIN, or touch. Store it securely outside the encrypted drive and keep a copy outside the PC. Check the actual saved file and test recovery before removing a working protector. A public certificate export is not a replacement for the lost private key.

Unplugging does not lock the drive

Once unlocked, the volume stays accessible until it is locked or the system shuts down. Automatic unlock must remain disabled if you expect an explicit key operation. Save work and close programs before locking; avoid forced dismounts during routine testing.

What was actually tested?

On Windows 11 Enterprise build 26200, a YubiKey 5C NFC with firmware 5.8.0, Minidriver 5.0.4.273 and Manager CLI 5.9.2: PIN-only unlocking, touch-only unlocking, recovery from the saved backup, and full XTS-AES 256 encryption passed. PIN ALWAYS plus touch ALWAYS did not pass BitLocker unlocking. PIN ONCE plus touch, presence-only, NFC, another PC and a full second clean setup remain untested. No policy selector reads or verifies your computer.
